# Hi, I'm Sushil Janardan 👋

### Ph.D. Researcher | Hyperspectral Imaging | Remote Sensing | Artificial Intelligence | Quantum-Inspired AI

I am a researcher working at the intersection of **Remote Sensing, Hyperspectral Image (HSI) Analysis, Artificial Intelligence, and Quantum-Inspired Computing**.

## 🚀 Research Areas

### Hyperspectral Image Classification

Development of deep learning architectures for extracting discriminative **spectral, spatial, and spectral–spatial features** from hyperspectral imagery.

### Hyperspectral Target Detection

Research on target detection frameworks using spectral information, spatial context, attention mechanisms, and target-aware feature representations.

### Quantum & Quantum-Inspired AI

Exploring quantum and quantum-inspired computational modules for hyperspectral image analysis, including hybrid classical–quantum architectures and quantum-inspired feature learning.

### Explainable Hyperspectral AI

Developing interpretable AI frameworks to understand the spectral and spatial characteristics responsible for model predictions.

### Remote Sensing for Precision Agriculture

Applying hyperspectral remote sensing and AI for:

* Early crop stress detection
* Crop monitoring
* Agricultural land analysis
* Sustainable precision agriculture
* Decision-support systems
